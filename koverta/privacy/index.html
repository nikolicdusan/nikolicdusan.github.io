Koverta

Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

The short version: Koverta does not collect any personal data. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

  • Your budget lives on your devices and in your own iCloud.
  • We, the developer, cannot see your data.
  • Payments go through Apple. We never see your payment details.

Who we are

Koverta is an envelope budgeting app for iPhone made by an independent developer based in Serbia. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean the developer of Koverta. For any privacy question, contact daftkeed@gmail.com.

What data Koverta handles

Koverta stores the budget information you enter: budget and envelope names, accounts, transactions (amounts, dates, payees, notes), exchange rates you type in, and the display name you choose to show next to your transactions.

This information is stored:

  • On your device, in the app's private storage.
  • In your iCloud account, if you're signed in to iCloud, so it syncs between your devices. It is stored in your private iCloud database, operated by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We have no access to it.

We do not operate any servers, so your data is never sent to us.

Sharing a budget with someone

If you invite someone to a budget, Apple's iCloud sharing makes that budget, including its transactions and the display names attached to them, visible to the people you invite, and lets them edit it. You can see who has access and stop sharing at any time in Koverta (Budget → people icon → Manage sharing). When you stop sharing, the budget is removed from their devices.

Purchases

Koverta Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your name, card or billing details. The app only checks with Apple, on your device, whether you have an active Pro purchase.

Siri and Shortcuts

If you use Siri or the Shortcuts app with Koverta, your requests are handled by Apple under Apple's Siri privacy terms. Koverta receives only the details needed to add the transaction (such as the amount and envelope) and does not send them anywhere.

Exporting your data

You can export all your transactions as a CSV file at any time (Budget → people icon → Export transactions). The file goes only where you choose to send or save it.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of data with anyone.
  • No profiling and no artificial intelligence processing of your finances.
  • No accounts, no email sign-up, no contacts or location access.

Deleting your data

  • Deleting the app removes the data stored on that device.
  • To remove the copy in iCloud: on iPhone, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Koverta → Delete Data.
  • If someone shared a budget with you, the owner can stop sharing, or you can remove yourself from the share in Koverta.

Your rights

Because we don't collect or store your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side: you control all of it directly on your devices and in iCloud. If you have any question about your data or this policy, including under the GDPR or Serbia's Law on Personal Data Protection, email daftkeed@gmail.com and we'll reply.

Children

Koverta is intended for adults managing a household budget. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. If a change affects how your data is handled, we'll also mention it in the app's release notes.
