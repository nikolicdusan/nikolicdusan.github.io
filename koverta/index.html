How do I invite my partner?
  1. Make sure you're both signed in to iCloud on your iPhones.
  2. In Koverta, tap the people icon at the top of the Budget tab.
  3. Tap Invite partner and send the link however you like.
  4. Your partner taps the link and the shared budget opens in Koverta on their phone.

Sharing is part of Koverta Pro. Only the person who invites needs Pro.

My partner's changes don't show up

Check that both phones are online and signed in to iCloud. Then pull down on the Budget screen to refresh. Changes usually appear within a few seconds.

What's free, and what's in Koverta Pro?

Free: a full envelope budget with up to 15 envelopes, transactions, accounts, Siri and CSV export. Pro adds partner sharing, unlimited envelopes and multiple currencies. Pro is available monthly, yearly (with a 14-day free trial) or as a one-time lifetime purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions, pick Koverta and tap Cancel Subscription. If you cancel during the free trial, you won't be charged.

I bought Pro but it's not active

In Koverta, tap the people icon at the top of the Budget tab, then Upgrade to Pro, and choose Restore purchases at the bottom. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

Does Koverta connect to my bank?

No, on purpose. You enter spending yourself, which takes seconds and keeps you aware of where the money goes. It also means your bank details never leave your bank.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app to remove data stored on your phone. To remove the iCloud copy, open Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage, pick Koverta and delete its data.
